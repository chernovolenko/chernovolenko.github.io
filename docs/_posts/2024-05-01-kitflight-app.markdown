Building an experimental aircraft is an exciting and complex journey. Keeping detailed logs of your build process is not only crucial for staying organized but also important for meeting requirements during certification. That’s where Kitflight, the mobile-first log app for kit planes, comes in.

### Why Do You Need a Logging Tool for Your Kit Plane?

As a kit plane builder, tracking your progress—from time spent on tasks to categorizing different stages of your build—is key to success. A detailed logbook helps you stay on top of your project, whether you’re working on fuselage construction, electrical wiring, or avionics installation. Additionally, accurate documentation is needed for FAA certification processes. 

Kitflight is specifically designed for experimental aircraft builders, offering a streamlined way to manage your logs directly from your mobile devices. Unlike traditional logging systems that are limited to desktop or web apps, Kitflight is mobile-first, allowing you to document progress right from the shop floor with or without internet connection. Whether you’re attaching photos to your logs, tracking time spent on tasks, or adding detailed annotations, Kitflight makes sure all your work is in one place and easily accessible.

### Key Features of Kitflight App

* **Track Time Spent on Tasks**: Effortlessly log the time you spend on each part of your build.
* **Attach and Annotate Photos**: Add annotations to photos to make work logs more descriptive.
* **Organize Your Build**: Categorize your work for a more organized and structured log.
* **Search Your Logs**: Use advanced search features to quickly find specific entries.
* **iCloud Sync**: Access your logs across all devices with seamless iCloud integration, so your data is always up-to-date.
* **Easy to start**: No account needed. No special requirements. Ready to use.

### Why Choose Kitflight Over Self-Made or Other Logging Apps?

Kitflight is mobile-first. This means you can log your build from anywhere, without needing to return to your desktop to update your records. With Kitflight, your logbook is always in your pocket, whether you’re in the workshop or on the go.

### Designed for Experimental Aircraft Builders

Kitflight was designed with the unique needs of kit plane builders in mind. Its intuitive interface ensures that you can focus on your build rather than figuring out how to manage your logs. Plus, its iCloud sync feature ensures your logs are automatically updated across all your mobile devices, so you never have to worry about losing your data.

### How Kitflight Helps You Meet Certification Requirements

Experimental aircraft builders must document the construction process to meet certain certification requirements. Kitflight makes it easy to log each step of the process, so when the time comes to show your logs, you have a well-organized, detailed record of every task, accompanied with photos, and time spent.
