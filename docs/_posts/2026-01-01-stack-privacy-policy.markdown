# Privacy Policy – Stack

**Effective date:** 1 Jan 2026

Stack (“the App”) is designed to let users create and edit before-and-after photos and videos. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Stack is an offline-first application. All data you create or import is stored locally on your device.

### Local Data (On-Device Only)
We may store the following locally:
- Photos and videos you import into the App
- Projects, edits, and exports you create
- App settings and preferences

This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly share or export it.

---

### Analytics Data (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve performance and features.

The analytics data collected may include:
- App usage events (e.g. feature interactions, screen views)
- Device type and operating system version
- App version
- General performance and crash information

We explicitly do **not** collect:
- Photos or videos
- User-generated content
- Precise location data
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA is not used)
- Data used for advertising targeting

Firebase Analytics is configured without ad personalization or advertising identifiers.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use analytics data to:
- Improve app performance and stability
- Understand feature usage
- Fix bugs and crashes
- Guide product development

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

---

## 3. Data Storage

- All user-generated content is stored locally on your device.
- We do not operate servers that store user content.
- Analytics data is processed by Firebase in accordance with Google’s privacy policies.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service:

- Firebase Analytics (Google)

Firebase Analytics is used for anonymous usage analytics and crash reporting without advertising identifiers.

You can review Google’s privacy policy here:  
https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share personal content or user-generated media.

Aggregated and anonymized analytics data may be processed by Firebase solely to improve the App.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- Local data remains on your device until you delete the App or remove the content manually.
- Analytics data is retained according to Firebase’s default retention policies.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Because Stack does not store user content on external servers, you maintain full control over your data:
- Delete the App to remove all local data
- Manage analytics preferences via device settings where applicable

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Stack does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 and is not directed toward children.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and/or within the App Store listing.
